Our story

Groceries, without the traffic

FreshCart started in 2019 with one Suzuki van, a cool-box and a simple promise: get mandi-fresh produce to Karachi kitchens before lunch. Today we run three cold-chain hubs and deliver to seven cities — and the promise hasn't changed.

🌿 Sourced at sunrise

Our buyers are at the sabzi mandi by 4 AM. Whatever doesn't meet the bar simply doesn't get loaded.

❄ Unbroken cold chain

Dairy, meat and frozen items stay between 0–4°C from the hub to your doorstep.

🤝 Fair to farmers

We buy direct from 140+ growers in Sindh and Punjab and pay within 48 hours.

♻ Less plastic

Paper crates and reusable cool bags — riders take the bag back on your next order.
